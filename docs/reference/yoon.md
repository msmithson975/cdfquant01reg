# Yoon Data Set

The data are records of 894 patients whose times were recorded for five
stages of ED assessment and treatment from a study consisting of
recorded information on patients admitted to the emergency department of
the University of Alberta Hospital between midnight January 23 and
midnight January 29, 1999 (Yoon, Steiner, and Reinhardt 2003).

## Usage

``` r
yoon
```

## Format

A data frame with these columns:

- pphysdecis:

  The proportion of total time spent by each patient in the ED under
  assessment and treatment by a physician

- Ambulance:

  A binary variable indicating how each patient arrived at the ED, 0 =
  walk-in, 1 = ambulance arrival

## Source

Yoon, P., I. Steiner, and G. Reinhardt. 2003. Analysis of factors
influencing length of stay in the emergency department. CJEM 5
(3):155–61.
